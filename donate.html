Before You Donate

Four small steps for a smooth donation.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before and after your donation to help your body feel its best.

Eat a Healthy Meal

Have a balanced, iron-rich meal a few hours before donating to keep your energy steady.

Bring Identification

A valid ID helps the blood center verify your identity and register your donation.

Follow Medical Eligibility Guidance

Answer screening questions honestly so professionals can confirm you are ready to donate.

Note: Always follow the guidance of qualified healthcare professionals and blood centers. This page does not provide medical advice or eligibility decisions.
