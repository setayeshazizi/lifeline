About Lifeline

Technology That Connects People When Every Second Matters.

Lifeline is a student-built demonstration platform that shows how thoughtful, simple technology can help close the gap between those who need blood and those willing to give it.

Our Mission

Why Lifeline exists.

The Problem

Blood shortages and the difficulty of finding compatible donors quickly can create serious challenges during emergencies. Families often spend critical hours calling hospitals and searching for the right blood type — time that patients simply don't have.

The Solution

Lifeline creates a digital connection between donors, blood requests and blood centers. One clear dashboard shows what is needed, where, and how urgently — so the right people can be matched with the right request in minutes, not hours.

The Journey

One request, five moments of hope.

  1. 1

    Request

    A hospital, blood center or family submits an urgent request with the blood type, component, location and urgency.

  2. 2

    Match

    Lifeline filters its donor network and highlights compatible, nearby donors and available blood stock.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Coordinators contact the matched donor or blood center and arrange the fastest possible handover.

  4. 4

    Donate

    The donation takes place at a verified blood center, following all professional medical screening procedures.

  5. 5

    Support

    The patient receives support, the donor gets confirmation, and one more life moves forward.
